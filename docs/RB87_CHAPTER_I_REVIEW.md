| Inside one Rb87 atom | §§1, 2.1, 3; PDF pp. 2–4 | [NIST atomic reference data](https://www.nist.gov/pml/atomic-reference-data-electronic-structure-calculations/atomic-reference-data-electronic-7-36) gives neutral Rb as `[Kr]5s¹`; [NIST Rb I levels](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/rubidiumtable5_a.htm) lists the 5s ground term. | Whole atoms as bosons | §4 and §4.1; PDF p. 4 | Chapter I defines symmetric exchange and occupation of *whole-atom motional modes*. The original [1995 Rb87 condensate report](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.269.5221.198) confirms the experimental system was a dilute vapor of Rb87 atoms. The Library does not present a shared motional mode as electrons sharing an electronic shell. |
| What condenses | §5; PDF p. 5 | The original [Penrose–Onsager paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.104.576) defines condensation through an extensive largest eigenvalue of the one-particle reduced density matrix. The Library describes this as a criterion, not an observable calculated by the current Lab. The finite dimer stores selected eigenstates but does not calculate a condensate fraction or Gross–Pitaevskii dynamics. |
